Why AJ Lab exists

01 / Mission

Most UI work stalls between the mockup and the build. A design looks finished in a file and falls apart the moment real data, real states, and real edge cases show up. We close that gap by designing with the constraints of the product from day one.

We work in small, direct teams — no account layers, no hand-offs that lose context. The person who designs the flow is the person who checks it in the browser. That's how interfaces stay coherent as they grow.

How a project runs

03
